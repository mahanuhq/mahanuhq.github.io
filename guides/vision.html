Mahanu vision

This is the product north star, not a list of features already shipped.

We are building Mahanu first for our own projects: studying, drawing, making music, building software, and creating things with people we trust.

These projects often end up split across chat, cloud documents, calendars, bookmarks, and one person's laptop. Mahanu should give the group one calm home for the work while leaving the files in forms they can keep and understand.

The project rule

A project helps its members get better at a shared pursuit: a subject, skill, craft.

A study circle, comic, garage band, drawing practice, robotics team, family project, or software project fits. A project of one fits too. The rule is about purposeful work, not artistic quality, or status, or popularity, or endless time-sinks.

The Acceptable Use Policy sets the binding safety floor. The philosophy guides harder product and listing choices.

What Mahanu should make possible

  • Begin with an idea and a useful starter.
  • Gather sources, notes, plans, schedules, decisions, and finished work.
  • Work alone, asynchronously, or live when the group chooses.
  • Keep going offline and converge after reconnecting.
  • Understand history and recover mistakes without learning Git.
  • Use readable files and normal tools on desktop.
  • Add full project copies on servers, desktops, web, and mobile as supported.
  • Publish selected work without exposing the rest of the project.
  • Learn from other projects without building a social feed.

Over time, a project becomes a portfolio of both the result and the process: drafts, decisions, practice, and improvement.

What Mahanu will not become

  • a feed optimized for attention;
  • a network of public personal profiles;
  • behavioral advertising or data sales;
  • a place where sponsors control projects, rankings, moderation, or product decisions; or
  • a proprietary format that traps the work.

The full stack is MIT-licensed so people may self-host, continue, or fork it.
