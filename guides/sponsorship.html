Advertising, sponsorship, and support

Future policy. Advertising, sponsorship, grants, affiliate links, and the Support and Resources directory are not live.

Mahanu may use ethical commercial support to fund the service and help projects. It will not copy the behavioural advertising or attention systems of social media.

Permanent boundaries

Mahanu will not:

  • build advertising profiles of people;
  • infer a person’s interests for advertising;
  • use unpublished project data for background advertising or sponsorship matching;
  • use passphrase-protected data for project assistance or commercial matching;
  • sell or give sponsors project data, member data, inferred support needs, or audiences;
  • use feeds, followers, popularity, or engagement to place commercial content;
  • let payment change organic search, Explore ranking, moderation, or project access;
  • place commercial content on user-created project pages, inside the editor, history, assistant, notifications, or recovery;
  • use third-party tracking pixels or cross-site advertising trackers; or
  • show commercial content to supervised accounts.

Signed-out visitors may see general-audience commercial support based only on the page or search they are viewing. Mahanu does not use an account, project, device, session, history, or profile to choose it.

An independent account must attest that its holder is an adult before it can ask for sponsored support, save an offer, submit an application, or make another commercial disclosure. Attestation is self-declared, not age verification.

Where commercial content may appear

Mahanu may place it only on pages built and controlled by Mahanu:

  • Explore;
  • Mahanu search; and
  • Support and Resources pages.

Paid content uses a separate slot and a clear label such as Advertisement or Sponsored. It never looks like an ordinary project, search result, recommendation, or independent Mahanu opinion.

Mahanu may launch with fewer surfaces. A sponsor has no right to a placement, category, renewal, or minimum exposure.

Background matching without profiling people

Mahanu may choose relevant support using:

  • the current Explore or search category;
  • words an adult enters in a search;
  • a project’s public name, tagline, and purpose; and
  • work the project has deliberately published.

Mahanu may infer what support systems a project could use, such as materials, payments, polls, calendars, research, or specialist help. That inference stays inside Mahanu. It is not a user-interest profile and is not sent to the sponsor.

Background commercial matching does not use unpublished notes, files, assistant use, reading history, or member activity.

Project assistance and requested sponsor searches

If an owner enables project assistance, Mahanu may read unpublished, unencrypted data to create support material for that project and generic, unattributed templates, examples, research, and guidance. This is a project-level setting. It does not permit Mahanu to edit the project or use that data for commercial matching.

An attested independent adult may explicitly ask Mahanu to find sponsored support for a project and select the unencrypted project material used for that search. This is a separate one-time request, not a consequence of enabling project assistance. The source data and inference stay inside Mahanu. The sponsor gets nothing unless an authorised adult submits an application.

Passphrase-protected data is never used. Mahanu does not infer a person's interests.

Allowed forms

After manual review, Mahanu may offer:

  • labelled contextual advertisements;
  • support cards for grants, discounts, services, resources, workshops, competitions, or equipment;
  • direct grants that a project owner chooses to apply for;
  • clearly disclosed affiliate links; and
  • sponsor-funded templates, research, events, public data, or open-source features.

A sponsor may fund work but cannot control Mahanu’s conclusions, product design, priority, moderation, or continued maintenance. Mahanu decides what to build and must disclose the funding where people see the resulting work.

Grants and applications

A grant or offer must state who provides it, what it provides, eligibility, terms, and the commercial relationship.

The sponsor receives only the fields and attachments an authorised owner submits. A guardian may apply for a project that includes a child, but the offer is shown only to the adult. The sponsor receives no separate child data and gets no direct route to the child.

Affiliate commissions and measurement

An affiliate link may use one generic Mahanu referral code. It must not contain a user, account, project, search, or session identifier. Mahanu does not record ad impressions, clicks, or conversions and gives sponsors no campaign or audience report.

The sponsor may provide only the aggregate amount needed to calculate and pay Mahanu’s commission. The link clearly says that it leaves Mahanu. The sponsor’s site follows its own terms and privacy policy.

Ordinary security logs are not used or shared for advertising measurement.

Approval and removal

Mahanu starts with a narrow manual allowlist. Every sponsor, offer, claim, destination, and funded item is reviewed before publication.

Mahanu may reject, restrict, pause, or remove any sponsor, category, offer, or placement. It may add stricter rules later. Payment does not guarantee renewal or prevent removal.

Before launch, Mahanu must provide clear labels, general-audience signed-out delivery, attestation-gated commercial interactions, isolated paid slots, reporting, takedown, and appeal. Sponsor agreements must preserve every boundary above and receive legal review for each launch country.
