If Mahanu disappears tomorrow

Mahanu should pass a simple test: if the company or hosted service closes, what can you still use?

What you keep

  • Readable work on a desktop. Markdown and standard media already in a desktop project folder remain usable in normal apps.
  • Downloaded copies. A readable folder downloaded earlier remains yours.
  • History in surviving full project copies. Reading that history may require compatible Mahanu software, but it does not depend on an account server.
  • The software. Mahanu is MIT-licensed. There is no license server or remote activation required to keep the code.
  • The option to self-host. A family, team, or fork may run compatible infrastructure.

What you may lose

  • work or history that existed only on Mahanu's servers and was never copied or backed up to your devices;
  • hosted login, sync, email, Explore, publishing, and support services; and
  • browser or mobile work that was not synced or backed up to your devices.

Reduce your risk

Add a desktop project copy, download a readable folder, and keep an independent versioned backup. The goal is not a last-minute export button. The goal is for your normal working copy to already be in a form you can keep.
