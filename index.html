why mahanu
✦

Yours, forever

open-format files on your own disk

flip

App saves files to your disk — opt-in storing on our servers, for backup, sync, publishing. All files are open-formats any computer can read.

✦

Edit live, together

edit with your circle at the same time

flip

No conflicted copies and no final_v2_FINAL. Everyone's change lands on everyone's device, even after being offline.

✦

Nothing lost

versioned snapshots

flip

See who changed what, revisit any earlier day, and restore anything. A restore is just another entry in the story.

✦

Safe by design

no likes, no followers, invite-only inside

flip

Only a project's card is public. Everything else — whether it sits on your disk or our server — is always private to your team.
