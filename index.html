why mahanu
✦

Yours, forever

your files, your disk, your apps

flip

Save your files to disks you own. Open files with Mahanu or any app of your choice. Opt-in for server backup, sync, publishing.

✦

Work together

online or offline, in Mahanu or your own app

flip

Work online together or sync after your work. No need for copies of copies. No final_v2_FINAL. Most files just converge across devices.

✦

History that is useful

versions, named checkpoints, tested restore

flip

See how the work changed, preserve important versions, restore any version.

✦

Safe by design

shared with only those you care, publishing is a choice

flip

Project membership, public listing, per file protection, and publishing are separate choices that you can decide.
