How this book is reviewed

This book is a fantasy on purpose, but the science in it is on the record. Before a page is published it goes through two reviews. The chips on the book's page link here so you can see exactly what each one means.

Reviewed by human editors

The project's users read every line before it goes live — checking the story against the science record and the style guide, and that nothing in the canon was broken. A human decides what publishes; the AI pass below only advises.

Editors: book_editors@mahanu.com

Reviewed by AI

An AI pass reads each page against the book's own rules and flags anything off — a science error, a tone drift, a continuity break — for a human to judge.

  • Model: Claude Opus 4.8 (Anthropic).
  • Checks against: this book's canon (the invariants), its in-world science record, and the art/style guidelines — the same rubric a human editor uses.
  • What it can't do: approve a page. It only marks; a human decides.

The prompt it was given

You are reviewing a page of Mahanu and the Entropy Warriors, a book where the fantasy is deliberate but the science must be correct. Check this page against the book's canon (the invariants), its science record, and its style guide. Flag, with the exact line: (1) any science claim that is wrong or uncheckable, (2) any break in the established canon or continuity, (3) any tone or voice drift from the style guide. Do not rewrite. Do not approve — a human decides. If a page is clean on all three, say so plainly.
