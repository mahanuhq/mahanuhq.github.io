Authority and versioning

If the public documents conflict, the Terms govern the hosted-service contract; the Privacy Notice governs personal-data processing; and the Acceptable Use Policy governs conduct and enforcement. The other documents govern their named subject. Product guides never silently amend them.

Each effective document must carry an effective date, a version, a plain-language change summary, and a durable archived copy. A material change requires advance notice and, where law requires it, renewed consent. The final footer must identify:

  • [Mahanu Operator legal name], registered address, and contact;
  • privacy@mahanu.com, legal@mahanu.com, and reports@mahanu.com contact routes;
  • applicable governing law, courts, and any required EU/UK representative or data-protection contact; and
  • the supported launch jurisdictions.
