Reporting & Appeals

Pre-launch draft · not yet in effect. The final version must name monitored report channels, response targets, escalation owners, local notice rules, and an accessible alternative way to report.

1. What to report

Use the report control on a public card, publication, advertisement, or support offer, or contact reports@mahanu.com, for suspected violations of the Acceptable Use Policy, child-safety concerns, impersonation, privacy disclosure, intellectual-property concerns, security abuse, bot misuse, or a commercial item that violates Advertising, sponsorship, and support.

Include the relevant URL or project identifier, a short explanation, and any information that helps us assess the concern. Do not include unnecessary sensitive material. Do not use Mahanu reporting for emergencies: contact local emergency services when someone faces immediate danger.

2. What happens next

We acknowledge reports and protect the reporter’s information where possible. We apply the relevant policy, take a response that fits the case, or explain why we cannot act. The final service must publish target response times for each severity. These are goals, not guarantees.

Possible actions are described in the AUP. We may preserve relevant records, seek clarification, restrict a public surface while reviewing it, or make an emergency safety/security intervention. We generally do not disclose private project content, a reporter’s identity, or another person’s personal data while resolving a report.

3. Notice and appeal

Where practical and lawful, we notify the affected person or project of the action, the rule involved, and the available review route. An affected account owner, project owner, guardian, reporter, or sponsor may ask for review through appeals@mahanu.com within [appeal window]. State the decision identifier, the reason for review, and any new information.

The same appeal route applies when a future automated check first raised the case. Where possible, a different trained person reviews the appeal. The final policy must state review targets, any outside dispute route required by law, and how to contact the right regulator. An appeal asks Mahanu to reconsider. It does not automatically restore access or public listing.

4. Transparency and misuse

Mahanu will publish the transparency information required by applicable law and may publish voluntary aggregate information when it cannot expose reporters, children, or private projects. Knowingly false, abusive, or automated reports may be limited or enforced under the AUP, without discouraging good-faith reports.
