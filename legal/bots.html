Bot and Automation Policy

Pre-launch draft · not effective. No bot or conduct-automation feature launches until its permissions, operator terms, data flow, retention, human review, and jurisdiction duties are approved.

1. What a bot is

A bot is an automated project member. It must be visibly labelled, tied to a responsible operator, granted an explicit role, and attributed in project history. Hidden or deceptive automation is prohibited by the Acceptable Use Policy.

Mahanu may operate a bot as part of the hosted service. A third-party bot is operated by someone else and follows that operator's terms and privacy practices.

2. Adding a bot

Before enabling a bot, Mahanu must show:

  • who operates it;
  • what permissions it requests;
  • what project data those permissions expose; and
  • whether the operator is Mahanu or a separate recipient.

An owner approves an optional bot. A future supervised account also requires guardian approval. Removing a bot ends future access but cannot recall data already received.

A bot operator must secure credentials, stay within granted permissions, collect only needed data, avoid profiling or resale, and never contact members outside approved project surfaces.

3. Conduct automation

Mahanu can inspect unencrypted project data stored on Mahanu servers to enforce the Code of Conduct. It cannot read passphrase-protected files; users self-certify that protected content follows the same rules.

Any future conduct bot or automated flagging system must define, before launch:

  • exactly which content it reads and why;
  • what it stores and for how long;
  • who receives a flag;
  • what serious categories are escalated;
  • how protected files are handled;
  • when law requires reporting or preservation; and
  • how a person reviews and an affected user appeals a decision.

Automation must not judge artistic merit, political viewpoint, or project quality. No account-level action is taken by automation alone.

4. Reports

Report bot misuse through Reporting and Appeals. A decision based on bot evidence is reviewed like any other hosted-service decision.
