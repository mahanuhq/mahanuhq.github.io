Acceptable Use Policy

Pre-launch draft · not yet in effect. This is the minimum safety policy for the hosted service. It is narrower than the cultural project rule. It does not let Mahanu judge quality, opinions, or a member’s private life.

1. Scope

This policy applies whenever someone uses Mahanu’s hosted systems. That includes accounts, bots and their operators, project data stored on Mahanu servers, public cards, published work, invitations, reports, assistant requests, and support applications. It is part of the Terms of Service.

The project rule explains what Mahanu is for. This policy says what is not allowed. Mahanu may inspect unencrypted data stored on its servers to enforce this policy. It cannot inspect passphrase-protected files. Mahanu does not judge artistic quality, political views, or lawful personal choices. Any future automated checks must follow the Bot and Automation Policy.

2. You must not use Mahanu to

  • sexually exploit or endanger a child; create, request, possess, or share child sexual-abuse material; or groom a child;
  • threaten, plan, direct, or help with imminent violence, terrorism, trafficking, or weapons intended to harm people;
  • expose someone’s private information, stalk, harass, extort, impersonate, knowingly make false claims that harm them, or share their sensitive information without permission;
  • commit or help with fraud, phishing, identity theft, unauthorised access, malware, credential theft, or evasion of security or payment controls;
  • infringe copyright, privacy, publicity, confidentiality, or other rights, including by publishing material you lack permission to share;
  • exploit a security flaw; disrupt the service; scrape beyond allowed access; bypass access controls; create deceptive accounts or invitations; run a hidden or deceptive bot; exceed a bot’s permissions; harvest member data; evade a safety control; or use automation that harms the service or its members;
  • use Explore, search, invitations, reports, grants, advertising, or support features to send unsolicited commercial contact, buy undisclosed preferential treatment or organic ranking, collect data, profile people, or run behavioural advertising; or
  • violate applicable law or use Mahanu to organise conduct that would do so.

3. Public and supervised surfaces

Public cards and published work must not identify a supervised member, child, school, precise location, or sensitive circumstance. A guardian controls the supervised account’s public-card, membership, publication, and external-provider choices. These controls supplement rather than reduce the prohibitions above.

Advertising, support offers, and grants must also follow Advertising, sponsorship, and support. A sponsor cannot purchase data, organic ranking, private access, or unsolicited contact with a project or member.

4. Enforcement

Mahanu may issue a warning, remove or hide public material, disable an invitation or feature, suspend an account, keep relevant evidence, or end hosted access. The response depends on context, harm, evidence, repeated behaviour, safety, and the law. Urgent safety, security, or legal risks may require immediate action.

Use Reporting & Appeals to report a concern or request review. This policy does not promise that Mahanu will remove content that it cannot verify, does not control, or is legally required to retain.
