Guardian Addendum

Pre-launch draft · not yet in effect. This applies when a guardian creates or manages a supervised account. It adds to the Terms and Privacy Notice. It does not claim to meet every child-privacy or safety law by itself.

1. Guardian authority and consent

By creating a supervised account, you confirm that you are the child’s parent, legal guardian, or another person allowed to decide for them. You allow Mahanu to use the information needed for that account and its chosen features. This use must follow this addendum and the Privacy Notice. Before launch, Mahanu must record the consent, enabled features, and way to withdraw consent.

Counsel must approve the verification method, direct notice, consent record, and age thresholds in every supported jurisdiction. Mahanu will not treat a guardian relationship as a substitute for any legally required verifiable parental consent, age assurance, school authorisation, or additional child safeguard.

2. Controls the guardian holds

The guardian approves devices, recovery, project memberships, outside join requests, Explore changes, publication, protected files, assistants, and bots. A supervised account has no independent email login, public profile, follower graph, automatic public listing, or direct messages.

No supervised-account or conduct-automation flow launches until counsel approves its consent, monitoring, reporting, preservation, and appeal rules. Any approved automation is governed by the Bot and Automation Policy, Privacy Notice, and Retention and Deletion Policy.

The guardian must use these controls in the child’s interests, keep recovery methods secure, review project membership and providers, and promptly revoke a lost device or unwanted access. Mahanu may require a guardian action before a high-risk change takes effect.

3. Privacy and outside parties

Mahanu does not show commercial content to a supervised account. It does not use a child’s content or service use for advertising, profiling, paid placement, or AI training. A guardian may seek a grant for a project with a child. The sponsor receives only the application the guardian or project owner submits and has no direct channel to the child.

Before the guardian enables a custom endpoint, personal API-key provider, third-party bot, or other external service, Mahanu must identify that the provider is outside Mahanu’s data commitments and ask the guardian to confirm the selected disclosure. The guardian may withdraw permission by disabling the route, subject to the Retention & Deletion Policy.

4. Review, withdrawal, and deletion

Use guardian@mahanu.com or the guardian controls to review the account, withdraw consent where allowed, request deletion, or report a concern. Mahanu will make a reasonable authority check and explain what happens to the child’s access and projects. Withdrawal cannot delete copies already held by authorised members or records Mahanu must keep by law.

The reporting and appeal route is here. If a guardian and a project owner disagree about a supervised account’s membership or publication, Mahanu may pause the affected access while it verifies authority and applies the service rules.
