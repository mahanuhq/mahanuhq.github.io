Retention & Deletion Policy

Pre-launch draft · not yet in effect. The final version must match the real databases, backups, payment records, service providers, and laws in every launch country. This is the main source for how long Mahanu keeps data.

1. Principles

We keep personal data and project data only for as long as necessary to operate the service, honour a member’s choices, secure the service, resolve a dispute, and meet legal obligations. Deletion from Mahanu cannot erase material another authorised project member already copied or published elsewhere.

The final schedule must be enforced by a tested deletion process. It must cover backups and service providers. A legal hold, security case, fraud investigation, or legal duty may delay deletion of specific records. Mahanu records the reason and ends the hold when it is no longer needed.

2. Proposed operational schedule

Record Proposed maximum Deletion action
One-time-password code and verification attempts expiry plus 24 hours automatically purge
Revoked, expired, or logged-out session token hashes 30 days automatically purge
Rate-limit and routine security logs 30 days, unless incident-related automatically purge or aggregate
Active account, membership, and project-permission records while the account/project remains active delete or anonymise after the account/project process below
Project content and assets until an authorised owner deletes them or the project/account is closed remove from active storage; expire from backups on their documented cycle
Future automated conduct evaluation [period or not retained] define before the feature launches; do not retain unflagged content by default
Conduct flags and escalation material [conduct record retention period] restrict access; delete after review unless law requires preservation
Account-deletion request 30-day recovery window close access, then delete/anonymise eligible account records within 30 further days
Reports, appeals, consent/invitation, and enforcement records 3 years after closure delete or anonymise unless a longer legal/safety hold applies
Billing and tax records the legally required period for the transaction isolate and delete when that period ends
Encrypted backup copies [backup retention period] cryptographically or operationally expire on the same documented cycle

The final service must publish and enforce its backup-retention period. The account-deletion flow must explain which shared-project material remains with other members and which legal or billing records cannot be erased immediately.

3. Requests and verification

Use privacy@mahanu.com or [deletion request URL] to request deletion or use another privacy right. Mahanu makes a reasonable identity and authority check. A guardian acts for a supervised account. Mahanu confirms completion or explains what it kept and why.

Project owners may remove a member’s access, but cannot erase the member’s independent account or copies already received. Removal stops future project delivery after enforcement; it does not require a protected-file rotation prompt. A user may separately rotate a file passphrase and DEK after a suspected compromise.
